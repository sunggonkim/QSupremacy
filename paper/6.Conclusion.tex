\section{Conclusion}

This draft defines the structure of an ATC-style systems paper for \SystemName. The central claim is that quantum supremacy should be modeled and analyzed as an application-level break-even condition against native HPC/ML paths under the same quality target. The paper scaffold now follows the logical structure of our accepted ScaleQsim and AURORA-Q papers: motivation, limitation, prior-work gap, modeling design, evaluation setup, breakdown, scalability, sensitivity, and conclusion. The next step is to implement the full \texttt{sklearn digits}, quantum kernel, and QNN/VQC workloads and replace the placeholders with measured results.
