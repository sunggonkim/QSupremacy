\section{Conclusion}

This draft defines the structure of an ATC-style systems paper for \SystemName. The central claim is that quantum advantage should be measured at the application level by comparing native HPC/ML paths against quantum-circuit application paths under the same quality target. The paper scaffold now follows the logical structure of our accepted ScaleQsim and AURORA-Q papers: motivation, limitation, prior-work gap, system design, evaluation setup, breakdown, scalability, sensitivity, and conclusion. The next step is to implement the full \texttt{sklearn digits}, quantum kernel, and QNN/VQC workloads and replace the placeholders with measured results.
