\section{Conclusion}

This draft defines the structure of an ATC-style systems paper for \SystemName. The central claim is that quantum supremacy should be modeled and analyzed as an application-level break-even condition against native HPC paths under the same quality target. Our first Perlmutter result runs 160 digits cases and shows that quantum kernels require a median 421.9$\times$ speedup over the measured simulation path to match native time, while QNN/VQC requires 64.9$\times$ but usually does not match native quality. This does not show quantum advantage today. It shows why the right systems question is a threshold question. The next step is to add practical workloads in ML, drug discovery, optimization, and scientific simulation so that the model addresses the real claims people make about quantum computing.
