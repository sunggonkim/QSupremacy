\section{Conclusion}

We present \SystemName for same-input, same-quality quantum-advantage modeling. The controlled digits sweep requires 421.9$\times$ for quantum kernels and 64.9$\times$ for QNN/VQC; the practical suite contains 3,552 cases and shows that, with 90\% quality-gap recovery, $10^4\times$ covers 54.9\% of HamSim cases and $10^5\times$ covers 57.1\% of molecule cases. The main lesson is to report an advantage frontier and architecture bottleneck taxonomy, not a yes/no slogan.
