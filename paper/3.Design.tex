\section{Design}

\SystemName turns an application comparison into a replaceable architecture contract. Its invariant is that the input, native deadline, quality target, application loop, and provenance do not change when one QPU mechanism is improved.

\subsection{Paired Evidence Record}

Figure~\ref{fig:design-overview} begins with one concrete input/configuration tuple. Both paths receive the same instance identifier, seed, transform, molecule, graph, or Hamiltonian and the same natural-unit quality metric. The native path records every valid non-quantum candidate and selects the fastest one satisfying the native target. The circuit path records construction and simulator time, output quality, gates, measurement groups, shots, evaluations, and raw source. Failed candidates remain visible. A stronger native method simply replaces $T_{\mathrm{native}}$ and moves the boundary outward.

Simulator execution recovers the exact circuit output and demand; it never sets a QPU latency. The record explicitly tags values as measured, modeled, stress, or future replacement. Controlled records provide paired quality; deployment-facing records strengthen only the native frontier; large distributed simulations provide only capacity evidence. This typing prevents a 256-GPU throughput result or a 40-qubit state-vector run from becoming a QPU-scaling claim.

Records are not independent applications. A structural configuration fixes the workload instance and algorithm settings; seeds and repeat rounds provide independent observations within that configuration. Family statistics therefore report records, structural configurations, independent instance/seed pairs, and repeats separately. Claim intervals use a hierarchical bootstrap over independent instance/seed units, and workload-balanced aggregates give each family equal weight.

\subsection{Quality-First Physical Inversion}

The first stage partitions every record into a noiseless quality pass, a failure with a measured recovery ladder, or a failure without recovery evidence. Representative records are then replayed at $10^3$, $10^4$, and $10^5$ shots with independent sampling seeds. Hardware-target eligibility requires at least 0.9 replicate pass probability, a measurement contract for the same record, and coverage of the full application loop. A fixed selected VQE or QAOA parameter has useful output-quality evidence but does not close its outer optimizer. Such records and all other quality failures report \emph{algorithm/representation first}; their physical timing is retained only as a conditional execution lower bound.

For an eligible record, source or compiled schedules expose three ready-width bounds: all predeclared work ready, one measurement-group wave ready, and one evaluation ready. If $P_s$ shot lanes exist but only $P_{\mathrm{ready}}$ repetitions can issue, $P_s^{\mathrm{eff}}=\min(P_s,P_{\mathrm{ready}})$. We populate $P_{\mathrm{ready}}$ from compiled Chem. groups, source-audited static ML/Chem./Opt. loops, or the single Sim. circuit. No adaptive optimizer or mid-circuit branch is invented.

The strict reliability contract splits an application failure budget $\epsilon_a$ equally among logical, rotation-synthesis, and distillation faults. Routed logical locations and all finite-shot repetitions select $d$; the number and precision of arbitrary rotations select T states per rotation and a compatible QDK factory protocol. For distance $d$, physical syndrome round $\tau_s$, $N_T$ T states, $F$ factories, and $P_s^{\mathrm{eff}}$ lanes, the principal terms are
\[
\begin{aligned}
T_{\mathrm{log}} &= W_{\mathrm{cyc}}d\tau_s/P_s^{\mathrm{eff}},\\
T_{\mathrm{fac}} &= 15N_Td\tau_s/F,\\
T_{\mathrm{dec}} &= N_{\mathrm{wave}}(L_{\mathrm{service}}+L_{\mathrm{comm}}).
\end{aligned}
\]
The model adds host-visible rounds and recomputes the overlap bracket from Section~II. Physical output includes data qubits, selected factory-protocol qubits, LSQCA's 176-cell supply envelope, syndrome bandwidth, and reaction-time correction storage. Factory-internal syndrome traffic, rotation anticommutation depth, and uncompiled adaptive traces remain explicit unsupported flags.

\subsection{Joint Target and Mechanism Inversion}

For each eligible record and resource $j$, \SystemName recomputes the complete model after a 10$\times$ improvement and after making that resource non-limiting:
\[
G_j^{10}=T/T_j^{10},\qquad C_j=T/T_j^{\infty}.
\]
$G_j^{10}$ determines the first useful marginal target; $C_j$ is its Amdahl-style removal ceiling. The next target is computed only after removing the first and re-evaluating overlap, saturation, and feedback. This avoids ranking components by isolated bars that cannot execute concurrently. A record already satisfying quality, reliability, and runtime is labeled advantage-reached rather than assigned an unnecessary target.

One-factor results are insufficient when factory supply, useful lanes, logical cycle, decoder reaction, native deadlines, and overlap interact. The joint design therefore uses a deterministic, marginally balanced space-filling set over dependency mode, 0.5/1/2$\times$ tolerance, $10^3$--$10^5$ direct shots and useful lanes, fixed or precision-derived T counts, 1\%/0.1\% failure budgets, $10^{-3}/10^{-4}$ physical error, 1--50,000$\times$ factory supply, 0.1/0.4-$\mu$s syndrome rounds, 1/5/63-$\mu$s decoder service, 0.5/1/2$\times$ native deadlines, and $\rho=0/0.5/1$. These points measure design coverage, not probabilities of future technologies. A phase cell reports the first-target mode, agreement count, runtime-pass count, successor, and removal ceiling.

Published mechanisms use the same invariant. Our LSQCA replacement reconstructs each workload's ordered logical-operand events, applies a two-entry computation-register policy, and charges point-SAM's $7\sqrt{n}$-beat load and reverse store. Only core cells and matched movement change; quality, reliability, rotations, factory demand, and native time remain fixed. A separate BOSS-compatible envelope consumes direct Chem. and QAOA interaction graphs to bound shuttle, SWAP, recooling, and readout under locality-first and congestion-stressed policies. It is not presented as the full BOSS compiler or as a universal QCCD multiplier.

\subsection{Typed Architecture Decision}

The output is not a scalar speedup. For record $r$, \SystemName emits
\[
\mathcal{D}(r)=\langle e,j_1,x^*,j_2,\{C_j\},\pi\rangle,
\]
where $e$ is the evidence state, $j_1=\arg\max_j G_j^{10}$ is the first compatible resource, $x^*$ is its first crossover, $j_2$ is the target after that crossover, $C_j$ is each removal ceiling, and $\pi$ resolves every term to measured, modeled, stress, or replacement provenance. The evidence state is one of quality-blocked, loop-blocked, FT-invalid, hardware-target, or advantage-reached. A blocked record returns algorithm/representation or loop closure as $j_1$ and leaves physical $x^*$ undefined; it cannot inherit a component target from another record in the same family.

For a scalable resource, $x^*$ is the smallest tested or solved multiplier at which its first-target identity changes or complete runtime reaches native parity. The successor is then recomputed from the new operating point, not copied from the original component ranking. At family level we report the count and agreement fraction of record-level decisions. Agreement is deterministic design coverage, not a probability that a future machine will occupy that point. This typed output is what separates an architecture decision from a simulator benchmark: it states what is blocked now, what change is useful first, how much change is needed, and what becomes limiting next.

A proposed component is evaluated by replacing only the events it owns and regenerating $\mathcal{D}(r)$. The proposal earns application-level priority only if it changes $j_1$, reduces $x^*$, raises a runtime-and-quality pass, or exposes a new successor without violating reliability or adding unmatched costs. A large local speedup with unchanged $\mathcal{D}(r)$ is reported as low application-level leverage. This rule admits future measured QPU traces, native rotations, decoders, layouts, and HPC--QPU runtimes without changing the paired application contract.

\noindent\textbf{Implementation and audits.}
Perlmutter records capture Python/CUDA/library versions, Slurm provenance, seeds, failures, and raw timing. Qiskit compiles routing and commuting groups; QDK and Qualtran cross-check selected logical circuits. Independent audits verify quality eligibility, schedule provenance, reliability/space, statistical units, joint phase coverage, replacement invariants, measured-versus-modeled labels, and paper claims. Every retained result resolves to JSON/CSV evidence and a deterministic regeneration script.
