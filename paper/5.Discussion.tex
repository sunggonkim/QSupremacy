\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}

\noindent\emph{What the results prove.}
\SystemName does not prove current quantum advantage. It proves a computer-architecture point: once task, quality target, and native baseline are fixed, advantage becomes a measured design-space boundary. That boundary differs by workload, so the result must say whether future effort should buy faster logical gates, more parallel shots, lower error/control overhead, stronger native co-design, or better algorithmic quality.

\noindent\emph{Baselines, workloads, and algorithms.}
The largest threat is an underpowered native baseline. We reduce it by selecting the fastest valid method among six ML classifiers, exact/heuristic optimization, dense/sparse chemistry solvers, and dense/sparse simulation solvers, then measuring how the frontier shifts when the native side is strengthened. Stronger natives still exist, including CNNs, boosted trees, tuned MILP/domain heuristics, tensor networks, projector QMC, and CCSD(T)-class chemistry references; adding them would usually move the frontier rightward. The suite is also not exhaustive: chemistry/simulation use small active spaces, QAOA uses fixed grids, and the 4--16 qubit measurements are not a deployment-scale chemistry or final-QAOA extrapolation. Better ansatz, training, encodings, and shots are represented by recovery, but they are not free because they can increase depth, evaluations, and host-QPU feedback.

\noindent\emph{Hardware projection and artifacts.}
\cuQuantum is instrumentation, not the future hardware stack. Real hardware adds logical gate time, readout, finite shots, feedback, mapping, routing, data loading, queueing, and error correction. Hardware-specific studies should replace $T_{\mathrm{error}}$ and $P_{\mathrm{shots}}$ with code distance, cycle time, logical-error budget, magic-state throughput, decoder latency, controller bandwidth, and device concurrency; energy and cost can be attached to the same records. Timing risk is reduced by bundled Slurm runs, workload medians, login smoke gates, and a warmup-separated gate with 12 measured trials, zero failures, and maximum quantum-runtime CV is 0.0400. The artifact audit verifies the 160-case digits summary, 3,552-case practical suite, advantage projection, taxonomy, 104-case chemistry gate, and repeat-timing gate from stored JSON/CSV/accounting artifacts; the current audit status is PASS.
