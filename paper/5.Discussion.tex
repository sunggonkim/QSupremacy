\section{Discussion and Future Work}

\noindent\textbf{What should be built first.}
The result is not a universal request for faster gates. ML and Opt. fail direct quality even without hardware noise, and the passing Chem. outputs do not close the VQE optimizer. Their first target is therefore an encoding, ansatz/search, or mitigation method that preserves the same application output and exposes its complete loop. Soft-information mitigation and postselected rotation preparation illustrate relevant cross-layer directions, but their extra samples, success probability, and control path must enter the paired record~\cite{zhou2025softqem,choi2023postselectedrotation}. For the 12 eligible Sim. records, the strict FT path is factory-first throughout the 1--100$\times$ region. The multi-million-factory equivalent should be read as a demand mismatch, not a floorplan recommendation: architects must reduce rotation demand, increase compatible state supply, or use a modality that avoids synthesized rotations before decoder or movement improvements can affect application time.

\noindent\textbf{A conditional architecture roadmap.}
The joint DSE identifies when the roadmap changes. After $10^4$--$5\times10^4$ factory-supply improvement, insufficient useful lanes make shot parallelism first; when lanes are provisioned, logical-cycle latency becomes first for the remaining cases. TraceQ and d'ArQ can expose dependency and control structure, while Pinball targets predecoding latency~\cite{traceq2026,darq2026,pinball2026}. BOSS, LSQCA, S-SYNC, and Cyclone target movement, storage, or QCCD organization~\cite{boss2025,lsqca2025,ssync2025,cyclone2026}. These mechanisms are valuable at the operating points that activate their matched events. In our small eligible circuits, LSQCA saves no core cells and movement erases six runtime passes; at larger logical width, its area crossover can reverse that conclusion. The inversion is therefore a way to select and falsify architecture proposals, not a replacement for their detailed simulators.

\noindent\textbf{How a component earns priority.}
An HPCA mechanism normally reports a local objective such as factory rate, decoder latency, memory density, shuttle count, or gate utilization. QArchGauge does not compare those unlike units directly. It attaches the mechanism to the same record, charges every new event introduced by the mechanism, and asks whether the typed decision changes. At the measured surface-code point, even making decoder or host service free yields less than 1.01$\times$ end-to-end speedup, while making factory supply free yields 4,102$\times$. A decoder or layout proposal is therefore not rejected as unimportant; it is scheduled after the upstream crossover unless it also reduces rotation supply, shot serialization, or logical work. Conversely, the matched LSQCA experiment shows that a locally denser design can lose application value when its access movement is charged. This gives architects a falsifiable burden of proof: identify the records and operating point that activate the component, replace its matched events, preserve quality and reliability, and demonstrate movement of first target, crossover, successor, or pass count. Reported component speedup alone is insufficient.

\noindent\textbf{Universal FT is one operating point.}
The strict physical result assumes surface-code execution and synthesized arbitrary rotations; it does not imply that every useful quantum accelerator should instantiate millions of identical factories. Native or analog rotation schemes can replace part of the T-state term, while neutral-atom and trapped-ion machines expose different transport, reconfiguration, gate-time, and error costs~\cite{bluvstein2024logical,bluvstein2026faulttolerant,pino2021qccd,toshio2026starmagic}. Analog Hamiltonian simulators are especially relevant to the eligible Sim. records because their application loop ends in one observable~\cite{georgescu2014quantum,daley2022practical}. QArchGauge evaluates such a modality by replacing only the supported rotation, movement, and reliability terms and rerunning the same inversion. The native deadline and quality gate do not move. This distinction matters: specialized hardware may reduce the physical burden for Sim., but it cannot repair the representation and outer-loop failures that block ML, Chem., and Opt. before hardware timing begins.

\noindent\textbf{HPC--QPU co-design.}
A QPU is an accelerator inside a classical scientific workflow. An integrated runtime should expose the native deadline, finite-shot quality progress, dependency-ready width, factory and decoder backpressure, and host-visible feedback rather than only circuit count. QFw and emerging HPC--quantum ecosystems provide the execution substrate for this contract~\cite{qfw2025,qfwsc25,ornlhpcecosystem2024,nerscquantumroadmap2025}. Perlmutter then serves two roles: it produces exact paired evidence before hardware exists, and it remains the competing native path when QPU traces arrive. Replacing a modeled term with a measured QPU trace preserves the comparison instead of rebuilding the benchmark around the new device.

\noindent\textbf{Limits and next evidence.}
The 4--20-qubit controlled suite prioritizes exact quality closure; the hardware-qualified set is only twelve 4--7-qubit Sim. records. The 36--40-qubit runs are shard-capacity probes, not distributed application demonstrations. Large Chem. lacks a matched VQE loop, Opt. lacks deployment-scale closed-loop QAOA, ML amplitude loading/readout remains unresolved, and no adaptive or mid-circuit dependency trace is claimed. The FT model also omits factory-internal syndrome traffic, rotation anticommutation depth, measured queue tails, and end-to-end energy. Future work must attach larger quality-closed applications, emitted logical schedules, power traces, and measured QPU output. Energy should compare the complete classical baseline with cryogenics, control, decoding, and factory operation rather than count only device gates~\cite{auffeves2022energy,fellousasiani2023fullstack}. Until then, favorable points are falsifiable architecture targets, not evidence of a realized advantage machine.
