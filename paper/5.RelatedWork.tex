\section{Related Work}

\noindent\textbf{Application benchmarks and native comparison.}
SuperMarQ, QASMBench, and MQT Bench characterize applications, circuits, and compilation~\cite{supermarq,qasmbench,mqtbench}; QED-C combines quality with time-to-solution, and QUARK compares classical and quantum implementations~\cite{qedcbench,quark2022}. A recent Nature perspective likewise asks benchmarks to expose the subsystem they measure~\cite{proctor2025benchmarking}. None inverts a measured same-input native deadline into first, crossover, next, and removal-ceiling QPU targets after direct finite-shot loop closure.

\noindent\textbf{FT estimation and critical-path evidence.}
Beverland et al. compile three scaled applications into size, speed, and controllability requirements~\cite{beverland2022}; QDK and Qualtran expose QEC, factory, and technology assumptions~\cite{azureestimator,harrigan2024qualtran}. TraceQ reconstructs dataflow, d'ArQ groups causal control, and Pinball and decoder studies quantify reaction latency~\cite{traceq2026,darq2026,pinball2026,khalid2025reaction}. \SystemName adds same-record quality and loop closure plus a measured native deadline before assigning any component target.

\noindent\textbf{QPU component architectures.}
BOSS, S-SYNC, Cyclone, and LSQCA optimize QCCD movement, memory, or surface-code density~\cite{boss2025,ssync2025,cyclone2026,lsqca2025}; factory, synthesis, and non-Clifford-state work attacks other terms~\cite{hofmeyr2025puremagic,silva2024multilevelfactory,chatterjee2025qspellbook,campbell2017synthillation,choi2023postselectedrotation}. Their gains are mechanism-specific. Our replacement changes only matched events and space, then recomputes the application path to expose both benefit and added movement or reliability cost.

\noindent\textbf{HPC simulation and integration.}
ScaleQsim, AURORA-Q, and \cuQuantum improve distributed state-vector execution~\cite{scaleqsim,auroraq,cuquantum}; QFw and laboratory roadmaps study hybrid gateways, scheduling, and CPU/GPU/QPU integration~\cite{qfw2025,qfwsc25,ornlbridging2026,ornlhpcecosystem2024,nerscquantumroadmap2025}. \SystemName instead uses leadership HPC to produce paired evidence and the native competitor, then removes simulator time before QPU projection. It is an architecture diagnosis contract, not a simulator or runtime scheduler.
