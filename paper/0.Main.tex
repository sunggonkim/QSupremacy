\begin{abstract}
Application-level quantum advantage requires a complete circuit application to beat a strong native HPC path on the same input and at the same output quality. Simulator throughput and isolated fault-tolerant resource counts do not reveal which QPU resource can move that boundary. We present \SystemName, an HPC-driven architecture diagnosis framework that first gates every paired record by direct finite-shot application quality and then inverts its native deadline through workload-valid schedules, case-level reliability, physical space, and coupled resource improvements. Perlmutter supplies 3,552 ML, chemistry, optimization, and simulation records spanning 222 structural configurations and 16 seeds per configuration; its GPU time is evidence cost, never a QPU clock. At the declared noiseless tolerance, ML and optimization have no passing records, while 48/224 chemistry and 256/512 simulation records pass. Direct 10,000-shot closure further leaves only 12 simulation records with a same-record, full-loop application contract; all other physical timings are conditional lower bounds. For these 12 records, a QDK-aligned 1\% reliability contract selects distance 13--15 and 79--86 T states per arbitrary rotation. Magic-state supply must reach 39,351--42,961$\times$ a 64-factory baseline, or 2.52--2.75 million factories, before another component can dominate; six records remain blocked by the non-factory floor. Across 384 deterministic joint-design scenarios, factory supply is the first target for all eligible records throughout the 1--100$\times$ region, then shifts to shot parallelism or logical-cycle latency as supply increases. A matched-event LSQCA replacement provides no core-area benefit at 4--7 logical qubits and its load/store movement removes all six otherwise runtime-favorable cases. Leadership HPC thus acts as an empirical oracle for falsifiable first, crossover, and next QPU architecture targets rather than evidence of present quantum speed.
\end{abstract}
